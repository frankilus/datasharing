QUADRADIUS

← back to the game

Objective

Wipe out the enemy squadron. You win when every one of your opponent's pieces has been destroyed (or when they resign). You start with 20 pieces each, lined up in two rows on opposite sides of a 10×8 board.

Taking a Turn

Every turn ends with a move: one piece travels a single square up, down, left or right — click it and click a highlighted square, or simply drag the piece with your cursor and drop it. Landing on an enemy piece crushes it — that's how you capture. You can never land on your own pieces.

Before you move, you may activate any number of powers from any of your pieces. Using a power does not end your turn — only moving a piece does. So a typical turn might be: fire a Trench, slap on Jump Proof, then march a piece forward to finish the turn.

Elevation

Tiles range from −1 (trench) up through ground level to +2 (wall height). A piece can climb at most one level per move, but can drop down any height. A piece at the bottom of a trench is stranded until the terrain changes (or it finds a transport power). High ground is also armor: an enemy two levels below you simply can't reach you.

Power Orbs

Small chrome orbs drop onto the battlefield at intervals. The two red LED bars on the control panel show how many rounds remain until the next drop and how many orbs will arrive. Move a piece onto an orb to collect a random power. A piece can hoard any number of powers, but loses them all if it's destroyed — so spread the wealth.

Using Powers

Click one of your pieces and its power inventory appears on the red LED screen. Click a power to fire it; hover it to read what it does. Powers with ROW / COLUMN / RADIAL in the name affect the activating piece's entire row, entire column, or the 3×3 area around it.

Powerup Chart

TRENCH — Sinks every tile in the line two levels; all pieces on it, yours and the enemy's, sink with it.
PLATEAU — Raises every tile in the line two levels; all pieces on it ride up onto the platform.
INVERT — Flips elevations in range: peaks become pits and pits become peaks.
FLATTEN — Resets every tile in range to ground level.
ACIDIC — Floods the tile under every enemy piece in range with acid; those tiles sink each round and dissolve into a hole after three rounds. Can't be fired with no enemy in range.
TRIPWIRE — Straps a blinking mine to every enemy piece in range; the moment a mined piece moves, it blows up. PURIFY defuses it.
INHIBIT — Enemy pieces in range can't use powers for their next three turns.
SPYWARE — Bugs enemy pieces in range so you can inspect their power inventories at any time.
PURIFY — Strips enemy pieces in range of all powers, bugs and enhancements.
LEARN — Copies every power held by your friendly pieces in range into this piece (they keep theirs).
BANKRUPT — Wipes every unclaimed Power Orb in range off the board and destroys every power held by enemy pieces in range.
WALL — Raises the entire line to maximum height into a barricade; all pieces on it ride up.
RAISE TILE — Jacks this piece's own tile up two levels.
MULTIPLY — Divides like a cell, spawning a power-less copy on an adjacent empty tile you pick.
RELOCATE — Beams this piece to a random empty tile. Great trench escape.
TELEPORT — Beams this piece to any empty tile you choose.
CLIMB — Permanent: a propeller lets this piece scale any elevation in one move.
JUMP PROOF — Shields this piece for 12 rounds: enemies can't land on it (holes, acid, bombs and mines still hurt).
SMART BOMB — Rains five missiles on random targets: hits on enemy pieces destroy them, hits on open ground crater the tile a level lower and vaporize any orb. Never hits your own pieces.
KAMIKAZE — Self-destructs, destroying every piece on the eight surrounding tiles and scorching the ground.
ORBIC REHASH — Re-drops every unclaimed Power Orb at a new random location.
GROW QUADRADIUS — Permanently increases every future orb drop by one.
SWITCHEROO — Swaps positions with an enemy piece of your choosing: activate, then click the target.

Other Rules

  • An inhibited piece flickers red and can't fire powers until the jam wears off.
  • A timed match gives each player 30 seconds per move; running out plays a random move for you. Toggle it in the menu.
  • If a player has no legal move and no usable power, they lose.
  • Type anywhere and hit ENTER to chat with your opponent.
