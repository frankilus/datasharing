QUADRADIUS

← back to the game

Objective

Wipe out the enemy squadron. You win when every one of your opponent's pieces has been destroyed (or when they resign). You start with 20 pieces each, lined up in two rows on opposite sides of a 10×8 board.

Taking a Turn

On your turn you do exactly one of these:

  • Move one piece a single square up, down, left or right.
  • Activate one power held by one of your pieces.

Landing on an enemy piece crushes it — that's how you capture. You can never land on your own pieces.

Elevation

Tiles can be raised into plateaus and walls or sunk into trenches. A piece can climb at most one level per move, but can drop down any height. A piece at the bottom of a deep trench is stranded until the terrain changes (or it finds a transport power). High ground is also armor: an enemy two levels below you simply can't reach you.

Power Orbs

Small chrome orbs drop onto the battlefield at intervals. The two red LED bars on the control panel show how many rounds remain until the next drop and how many orbs will arrive. Move a piece onto an orb to collect a random power. A piece can hoard any number of powers, but loses them all if it's destroyed — so spread the wealth.

Using Powers

Click one of your pieces and its power inventory appears on the red LED screen. Click a power to fire it; hover it to read what it does. Powers with ROW / COLUMN / RADIAL in the name affect the activating piece's entire row, entire column, or the 3×3 area around it.

Powerup Chart

TRENCH — Sinks every tile in range two levels, stranding enemies at the bottom.
PLATEAU — Raises the tile under each of your pieces in range two levels.
INVERT — Flips elevations in range: peaks become pits and pits become peaks.
FLATTEN — Resets every tile in range to ground level.
ACIDIC — Tiles in range corrode, sinking one level each round until they dissolve into holes that destroy whatever stands on them.
TRIPWIRE — Rigs empty tiles in range with hidden wires; the first enemy to step on one is destroyed.
INHIBIT — Enemy pieces in range can't use powers for their next three turns.
SPYWARE — Bugs enemy pieces in range so you can inspect their power inventories at any time.
PURIFY — Strips enemy pieces in range of all powers, bugs and enhancements.
LEARN — Copies every power held by enemy pieces in range into this piece.
BANKRUPT — Destroys every power held by enemy pieces in range.
WALL — Raises empty tiles in range three levels into barricades.
RAISE TILE — Jacks this piece's own tile up two levels.
MULTIPLY — Spawns power-less copies of this piece on every adjacent empty tile.
RELOCATE — Beams this piece to a random empty tile. Great trench escape.
TELEPORT — Beams this piece to any empty tile you choose.
CLIMB — Permanent: this piece can scale any elevation in one move.
JUMP PROOF — Shields this piece for 12 rounds: enemies can't land on it (holes, acid, bombs and wires still hurt).
SMART BOMB — Blasts four random tiles into holes, destroying pieces on them. Never hits the launcher.
KAMIKAZE — Self-destructs, destroying every piece on the eight surrounding tiles and scorching the ground.
ORBIC REHASH — Re-drops every unclaimed Power Orb at a new random location.
GROW QUADRADIUS — Permanently increases every future orb drop by one.
SWITCHEROO — Swaps positions with an enemy piece of your choosing: activate, then click the target.

Other Rules

  • An inhibited piece flickers red and can't fire powers until the jam wears off.
  • A timed match gives each player 30 seconds per move; running out plays a random move for you. Toggle it in the menu.
  • If a player has no legal move and no usable power, they lose.
  • Type anywhere and hit ENTER to chat with your opponent.
